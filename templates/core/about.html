Our Story

Built to Make Businesses Profitable

SP-Tech Software Solution was founded in 2024 with a clear mission: empower small and medium businesses with software that helps them grow— not tools that slow them down. Our flagship product is SanjivaniOne ERP.

Our goal is simple: make companies profitable using software. We help increase revenue by replacing old, slow systems with new, fast solutions that teams can use on any device, anywhere. AI enablement sits at the core of everything we build.

A dedicated team of 10+ professionals delivers quality on time, and we stand behind every product with after-sales service and on-call support— so your success doesn’t stop at go-live.
